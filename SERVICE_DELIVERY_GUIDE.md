# SIX3 Agency Service Delivery Guide

## Overview
This guide outlines the complete service delivery process for SIX3 Agency's three core AI-powered marketing services.

### 1. Search Marketing & Paid Media
**AI-driven search marketing with advanced algorithm optimization**

#### Service Capabilities
- Multi-platform campaign management (Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok)
- Real-time AI bid optimization
- Advanced audience segmentation
- Competitor analysis and market research
- Custom attribution modeling
- Automated reporting and alerts

1. **Discovery & Strategy** (Week 1)

2. **Setup & Launch** (Week 2)
   - Account setup across platforms
   - Campaign creation and configuration
   - Tracking implementation

3. **Optimization & Scaling** (Weeks 3-8)
   - Daily performance monitoring
   - Weekly optimization cycles
   - A/B testing execution
   - Budget reallocation based on performance

4. **Ongoing Management**
   - Continuous AI-driven optimization
   - Monthly strategy reviews
   - Quarterly campaign expansions
   - Annual strategic planning

#### Key Performance Indicators
- Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)
- Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)
- Click-through Rate (CTR)
- Conversion Rate (CVR)
- Quality Score improvements
- Market share growth

#### Budget Requirements
- Minimum monthly ad spend: $5,000
- Management fee: 15-20% of ad spend
- Setup fee: $2,500 (one-time)
